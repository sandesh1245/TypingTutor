# Privacy Policy for ExamTyping Tutor

**Last Updated:** September 6, 2026  
**Application Name:** ExamTyping Tutor  
**Developer / Publisher:** ExamTyping Suite

## 1. Introduction
ExamTyping Tutor ("we", "our", or "the Application") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our desktop application handles user information when downloaded through the Microsoft Store or installed directly on Windows 10/11.

---

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect
ExamTyping Tutor is designed with privacy-first and offline-first principles:
- **No Keystroke Logging or Transmission:** The Application processes your typing inputs and keystrokes exclusively on your local device in memory to calculate speed (WPM) and accuracy. Keystrokes are **never** transmitted over the internet to any external servers.
- **No Personal Data Selling or Sharing:** We do not track, rent, sell, or disclose personal information or typing history to advertisers, brokers, or third parties.
- **No Third-Party Analytics / Ad SDKs:** The Application does not contain telemetry trackers, invasive marketing cookies, or third-party advertising SDKs.

---

## 3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device
All application data is saved locally on your personal computer in your user profile directory (`%APPDATA%\TypingTutor\`):
- **Candidate Profiles:** If you create an account, your username and password hash are stored in a local encrypted database on your machine. If you use "Guest Mode (Skip)", no account credentials are stored.
- **Typing Test History & Statistics:** Your completed mock tests, words per minute (WPM), accuracy percentages, keystroke error counts, and dates are saved locally so you can review your progress analytics offline.
- **Preferences:** Selected language keyboard layout (e.g., Remington GAIL, Mangal Inscript, Raavi, English QWERTY), exam duration, backspace mode, and sound volume preferences.

---

## 4. Internet Connectivity & Optional Features
- The core functionality of ExamTyping Tutor (all lessons, tests, 1,000+ passages, games, and analytics) operates **100% offline** without requiring an internet connection.
- In the event of optional license validation or user-requested online updates, standard secure HTTPS connections are used solely to verify purchase status.

---

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion
Because all data is stored locally on your machine:
- You retain complete ownership and control of your data at all times.
- You may delete all test history, profiles, and cached settings at any time by clearing the application data directory or uninstalling the app via **Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps**.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy
ExamTyping Tutor is safe for students and learners of all ages. We do not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children.
